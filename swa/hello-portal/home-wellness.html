FAQ

Short, shopper-friendly answers for a wellness storefront without turning the page into medical advice.

Why would someone use a sauna at home?

Many people use saunas as a relaxation ritual. Heat can feel soothing after a long day, may support a wind-down routine, and can make a home gym or recovery space feel more complete.

What are the benefits of cold plunges?

Cold plunges are often used for alertness, post-workout recovery routines, and the disciplined feeling of controlled cold exposure. They should be approached gradually and safely.

Should shoppers buy a sauna or a cold plunge first?

For comfort and frequent use, many shoppers start with a sauna. For athletes or people who already like cold showers, a cold plunge can be the first dedicated recovery item.

How does Amazon dropshipping fit this page?

The page can send shoppers to Amazon product pages while Amazon handles checkout, shipping, and fulfillment. This mockup uses search links until real affiliate or product URLs are ready.

Are saunas and cold plunges safe for everyone?

No. People with heart conditions, blood pressure concerns, pregnancy, fainting history, or cold sensitivity should talk with a clinician before using heat or cold exposure.
