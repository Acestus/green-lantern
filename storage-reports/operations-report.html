2. Naming Rules from CAF Guidance

Consistency over creativity: fixed pattern with predictable tokens.
Environment clarity: include dev/prd directly in name.
Regional traceability: keep region token (for example, usw2).
Identity intent: include suffixes like dat for data-plane access.
